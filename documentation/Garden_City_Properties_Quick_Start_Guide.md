**Login URL**: [https://www.gardencityproperties.net/admin/login/](https://www.gardencityproperties.net/admin/login/)  

---

### 1. LOGGING IN
1. Go to `https://www.gardencityproperties.net/admin/login`.
2. Enter your authorized admin **Email** and **Password**.
3. Click **"Sign In to Dashboard"**.

---

### 2. HOW TO EDIT AN EXISTING PROPERTY (SAFELY)
1. Find the property card under **"Live Properties in Database"** at the top of the dashboard.
2. Click the cyan **"Edit"** button (pencil icon).
3. The dashboard turns into an amber banner: `Editing Project: [Name] (Updating ID: [slug])`.
4. Update the text, prices, or unit layouts you wish to change.
5. **Images**: To keep the existing cover photo, leave the cover file selector blank! To add gallery photos, click "+ Add Photos".
6. Scroll to the bottom and click **"Save All Changes to Project"**.
7. Click **"Preview"** to verify on the live website.

---

### 3. HOW TO ADD A NEW PROPERTY
1. When you open Admin Pro, the form is ready in **"Add New Development"** mode.
2. Fill in **Section 1: Basic Info** (Project Name, Developer, Starting Price in OMR, Starting Area).
3. Fill in **Section 2: Narrative** (A 2–4 paragraph description).
4. Add **Section 3: Unit Configurations** (Click "+ Add Configuration" for each 1-BR, 2-BR, or Villa layout, upload its photo, and enter BUA sqm and price).
5. Add **Section 4: Location** (Paste Google Maps link and click "Auto-Resolve Location").
6. Select **Section 6: Amenities** (Check Infinity Pool, Gym, 24/7 Security, etc.).
7. Upload **Section 7: Media** (Select a 16:9 high-res cover photo and gallery slideshow photos).
8. Optional: Add YouTube walkthrough video in **Section 9**.
9. Click the large bottom button: **"Publish New Development"**.

---

### 4. 5 CRITICAL GOLDEN RULES FOR IMAGES
1. **Never upload .ICO files**: Only upload `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp`.
2. **Keep individual images between 500 KB and 3.5 MB**: Admin Pro automatically compresses them to high-speed WebP.
3. **Use 16:9 landscape aspect ratio** (`1920 × 1080 px`) for covers and gallery vistas.
4. **Give configurations their own photo**: Upload villa or apartment photos directly inside the configuration modal.
5. **Hard-refresh your browser** (`Ctrl + F5`) if a replaced photo doesn't immediately refresh on your screen.

---
